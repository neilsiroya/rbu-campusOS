\# RBU CampusOS — Product Definition



\## Product



RBU CampusOS is a student-focused digital operating system and social/information layer for Ramdeobaba University.



Tagline:



\*\*ONE CAMPUS. ONE IDENTITY. EVERY EXPERIENCE.\*\*



CampusOS exists because university information and student activity are fragmented across official websites, notices, WhatsApp groups, departments, social platforms, clubs, student communities, and informal networks.



CampusOS should unify that fragmented experience into one coherent student platform.



\## Product identity



CampusOS is NOT primarily:



\- an ERP

\- an LMS

\- an administrative portal

\- a generic student dashboard

\- a replacement for every official university system



Academic utilities are part of CampusOS, but they do not define CampusOS.



The product's core identity is the digital layer connecting campus life.

\## Product goal



CampusOS should become the default digital interface students use to understand what is happening around campus and access the tools, people, information, opportunities, and services relevant to university life.



It should reduce the need to search through multiple disconnected sources.



\## Official university information



Long-term, official RBU information should flow automatically into CampusOS.



Target architecture:



RBU Official Sources

→ ingestion / knowledge layer

→ normalized data

→ Supabase

→ CampusOS Feed / Events / Notifications / Search / Campus AI



Potential official sources include:



\- university announcements

\- circulars and notices

\- academic calendar information

\- news

\- events

\- achievements

\- other publicly available official university information



Prefer automation over manual duplication when reliable official sources are available.



\## Product principles



CampusOS should:



\- reduce information fragmentation

\- make campus discovery effortless

\- feel specific to RBU rather than generic

\- reward frequent use

\- surface relevant information quickly

\- connect previously isolated student experiences

\- remain understandable despite having many capabilities

\- prioritize actual student usefulness over feature quantity



Every major feature should answer a real student need.



Avoid adding features solely because they are technically possible.



\## Current development state



CampusOS is still under active development.



Demo data is acceptable where real production data is not yet available.



Demo data should not be removed merely to make screens empty.



Do not falsely represent demo information as verified real university data.
